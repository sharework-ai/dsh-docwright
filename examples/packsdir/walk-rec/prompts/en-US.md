# Walkthrough Record

Write one walkthrough record for the selected process (targetIndex across the plan's `chapters[].processes[]`).

## Output requirements

- `name`: copy the selected process's name verbatim.
- `title`: copy the selected process's title verbatim.
- `note`: at least 20 characters — what was walked and what was observed.

## Hard constraints

1. `name` must equal the selected process's `name` character for character.
